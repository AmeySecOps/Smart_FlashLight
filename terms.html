Using the light responsibly

SmartFlashlight is a convenience tool, not certified safety equipment. Don't rely on it as your only light source in an emergency, and don't point the strobe or bright light modes directly at anyone's eyes, at drivers, or at pilots.
  • Extended use of the torch or bright screen-light modes can warm up your device and drain the battery faster than normal use.
  • Strobe mode flashes rapidly. If you or anyone nearby has photosensitive epilepsy, avoid strobe mode.
  • SOS mode is a visual signal aid only; it does not contact emergency services on its own.
